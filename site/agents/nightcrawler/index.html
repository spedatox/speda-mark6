Mark III · OSINT & Surveillance

NightCrawler

NightCrawler is the system's eyes on the open web: finding, monitoring and corroborating public information about people, companies, events and trends. It owns the watchers and the News Desk, and it works from open sources only.

Corroborate, don't trust. A single source is a lead, not a fact.prompts/agents/nightcrawler/01_identity.md

How it operates

The doctrine, in practice

  • Cross-checks across independent sources, weighs their reliability, and separates what is confirmed from what is merely claimed, rumoured or contradicted.
  • Keeps the trail. Every finding carries its source — link, date, who said it. Intelligence the owner cannot trace back is treated as useless.
  • Watches rather than looks. A page, a feed or a topic the owner cares about becomes a standing watcher, not a one-off search.
  • Reaches past plain search with browser automation when a target does not yield to a query.

In the architecture

What is actually true of it

  • Owns the News Desk: an always-on zero-cost RSS tier with cross-outlet deduplication and keyword alerts, plus a quota-budgeted analyst tier.
  • Fronts the OSINT suite — IP geolocation and reputation, URLhaus, ThreatFox, MalwareBazaar, breached password checks, Shodan, OTX, dark-web search, email discovery, crypto tracing and IntelX.
  • Browser automation runs only inside container isolation on an internal network. The relevant MCP server carries a known CSRF advisory and is never exposed publicly.
  • Its watchers are the main consumer of the cheap-probe pattern — the deterministic scan is what decides whether an agentic turn is worth spending at all.

Its voice rule is one line long: no embellishment. Sourced, or not stated.
